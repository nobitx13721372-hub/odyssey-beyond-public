Nine miles from Göbekli Tepe, workers found a stone man with eyes of black stone. On the hill itself, the giants have none.

Same region, same age, about 11,000 years ago. The giants have arms, hands with long fingers, belts and fox-fur loincloths. Their heads are smooth blocks. So why no faces? Full film on YouTube, link in bio.
