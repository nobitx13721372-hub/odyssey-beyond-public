The pyramids were not built by slaves. In 2013, archaeologists found the diary of a man who worked on the Great Pyramid, written while he was doing the job. His name was Merer. For the next twelve minutes, you're on his crew: where you sleep, what you eat (bread, beer and a lot more meat than you'd think), how the stones crossed the Nile, how they went up, what the work did to your body, and why his diary ended up in a cave on the Red Sea.
