Why did people invent writing? Look at the last receipt you were handed. Five thousand years ago, in Uruk in southern Iraq, the oldest writing on Earth was mostly bookkeeping. Now you live there. Five moments of your life in the world's first great city, and five things the world was told about it, tested against the newest evidence (including a 2026 study of what people really ate).
